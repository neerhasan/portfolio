01 / About

Two trades, one person. I studied apparel manufacturing, freelanced as a designer for clients in 15+ countries, and now coordinate orders for international clothing brands.

My day covers sourcing, costing, quality compliance and buyer communication. Orders stay visible because I keep the reports and trackers senior management relies on.

Three years of graphic design means brand assets, marketing material and visual execution get done without an outside agency. Online you will also find me as Neer Hasan.
